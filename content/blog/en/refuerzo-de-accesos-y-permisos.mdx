## Strengthening Access and Permissions.

Cleaning a site is not enough.

If the door is left open, someone will come back in.

After an infection, reinforcement is mandatory.

---

### Why it happens

Many attacks repeat because:

- Passwords are not changed  
- FTP access remains active  
- File permissions are insecure  
- Unnecessary users exist  

WordPress recommends hardening the configuration after an incident.

Closing access points is just as important as cleaning.



---

### What it is

Reinforcement (Hardening) means:

- Changing all credentials  
- Applying secure permissions (proper CHMOD)  
- Limiting access to critical files  
- Deactivating unnecessary users  
- Protecting `wp-config.php`  

It’s not paranoia.  
It’s prevention.

---

### What it affects

If it is not reinforced:

- The attacker can return  
- New backdoors appear  
- The site enters a reinfection cycle  
- The hosting provider may block the account  

A cleanup without hardening is only temporary.

---

### Possible solutions

When I handle reinforcements at Alsnippets, I do:

- Total credential reset  
- User and role review  
- Correct permission configuration  
- Protection of critical files  
- Activation of basic monitoring
