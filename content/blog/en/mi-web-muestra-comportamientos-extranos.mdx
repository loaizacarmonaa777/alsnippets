## My Website Shows Strange Behaviors

Something doesn't feel right.

Your website loads, but sometimes it redirects.  
Users appear that you don't remember creating.  
Google shows strange search results.  
Your hosting sends you alerts.

And the most confusing part: when you log in as an administrator, everything seems normal.

When a website is attacked, the problems aren't always visible, but the risk remains active and growing.

---

## Why it happens

In most cases, it happens due to:

- Outdated plugins  
- Abandoned themes  
- Weak passwords  
- Misconfigured permissions  
- Poorly isolated hosting  

Attackers don't always seek to destroy your site.  
Sometimes they seek to **use your server as a tool**.

Google documents how to detect signs of compromised sites.



The problem is that many owners ignore the first warning signs.

---

## What it is

When I talk about "strange behaviors," I am referring to:

- Intermittent redirects  
- Hidden spam visible only to Google  
- Automatic file creation  
- Suspicious scheduled tasks (cron jobs)  
- Unusual increases in CPU consumption  

It’s not always a visible virus.  
Sometimes it’s a silent *backdoor*.

In other cases, it’s SEO spam, as I explain in this article about [Japanese Keyword Injection](/en/inyeccion-texto-japones-wordpress).

---

## What it affects

Ignoring these signs can lead to:

- SEO penalties  
- Partial de-indexing  
- Loss of trust  
- Hosting suspension  
- Data theft  

Furthermore, every day that passes, the attacker gains more control.

It’s not drama. It’s experience.

---

## Possible solutions

When I handle these cases at Alsnippets, I don't start by installing a random plugin.

First, I perform:

- Core file review  
- Comparison with official versions  
- `.htaccess` analysis  
- User review  
- Manual scan for obfuscated code  



Then I solve the root cause, change credentials, organize permissions, and produce a more secure environment.

I don't clean symptoms.  
I solve the entry point.
