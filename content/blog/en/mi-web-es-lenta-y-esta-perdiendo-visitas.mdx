## My Website is Slow and Losing Visitors.

You notice it in Analytics.  
Traffic is dropping.  
People enter… and they leave.

Your web takes too long to load.  
Sometimes 5 seconds. Sometimes more.

And today, that is far too long.

---

## Why it happens

The most common causes:

- Overloaded shared hosting  
- Unoptimized images  
- Unnecessary plugins  
- Overburdened database  
- Lack of caching  

Google confirms that speed is a ranking factor.

But it’s not just SEO.  
It’s real experience.



---

## What it is

A slow site is not just "heavy loading."

It is:

- Poor resource management  
- Unnecessary code  
- Excessive HTTP requests  
- Poorly optimized external scripts  

You can measure it with PageSpeed Insights.

But understanding it requires analysis.

---

## What it affects

A slow site:

- Reduces conversions  
- Increases bounce rate  
- Lowers Google rankings  
- Decreases trust  

Amazon proved years ago that milliseconds impact sales.

Your business is no different.
