## SEO and GEO?: Google, Context, and LLMs.

For years, SEO was taught like this:

- Repeating keywords
- Stuffing texts
- Forcing titles
- "Optimizing" densities

That worked… a long time ago.

Today, Google, Bing, and language models interpret **context, intent, and coherence**, not tricks.

Google itself emphasizes creating helpful content for people, not for algorithms. And updates like the Helpful Content Update made it clear that the focus has shifted.

If you are still writing for 2015 robots, you are out of the game.

---

### Why it happens

It happens because search engines have evolved.

They no longer rely solely on exact keyword matches. Now they evaluate:

- Semantic relationships
- Thematic depth
- Content structure
- Real-world experience signals

Furthermore, LLMs (Large Language Models) don't "read" the way indexing used to work. They analyze patterns, context, and structural clarity.



That is why technical SEO, content, and architecture can no longer be separated.

---

### What it is

Modern SEO —and what I call GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)— consists of:

- Building a clear architecture
- Organizing a correct semantic hierarchy
- Answering real questions
- Properly delimiting each topic
- Avoiding mixing concepts without order

It’s not about writing more. It’s about writing better-structured content.

When I approach SEO at Alsnippets, I don't start with keywords. I start by understanding the site's complete system.

---

### What it affects

Failing to adapt to this change results in:

- Loss of organic visibility
- Content that does not appear in rich results
- Lower probability of being cited by LLMs
- Traffic that does not convert

Today, it’s not just about "ranking." It’s about being interpreted correctly.

A poorly structured site confuses Google. And if it confuses Google, it confuses the user.



---

### Possible solutions

What actually works today:

- Defining architecture before producing content
- Creating clear and autonomous sections
- Writing in human language
- Addressing intent, not just terms
- Maintaining global thematic coherence

Additionally, optimizing for SEO and GEO implies thinking about:

- Scannability
- Structural clarity
- Depth without filler
- Authority based on experience

At Alsnippets, I organize SEO strategies as a system, not as loose actions. I perform audits where I analyze structure, context, and real opportunities.

Because optimizing is not about repeating. It is about structuring so that both humans and machines understand the same thing.
