## Technical Dependency Audit.

Every plugin promises to solve something.

Every external script promises to improve something.

But nobody talks about the cost.

Over time, the site depends on too many external pieces.

And that makes it fragile.

---

### Why it happens

It happens because:

- Plugins are installed without evaluating impact
- Third-party scripts are added without measuring load
- Compatibility is not checked
- Redundant tools accumulate

More features do not always mean a better site.

---

### What it is

Auditing dependencies means:

- Measuring the real impact of each plugin
- Evaluating resource consumption
- Reviewing external calls
- Identifying redundancies
- Determining what truly adds value

Google recommends evaluating external resources that affect performance.

Every dependency adds to the load.



---

### What it affects

Too many dependencies can cause:

- Slowness
- Internal conflicts
- Vulnerabilities
- General instability

And when something fails, nobody knows what caused it.

---

### Possible solutions

When I perform a dependency audit at Alsnippets, I do:

- A complete plugin inventory
- Individual technical evaluation
- Elimination of redundancies
- Strategic replacement when necessary
- Stability testing

Then I organize the ecosystem to be lighter and solve invisible overloads.

Less can be more.

You can also complement this with [Structural resource optimization](/en/optimizacion-estructural-recursos-wordpress).
