## WordPress Has a Bad Reputation… Unfairly.

WordPress powers more than 40% of the web.  
However, it continues to be labeled as slow, insecure, or unprofessional.

The reality is different: **WordPress doesn't fail; technical decisions do**.

---

## Common Mistakes That Ruin WordPress

- Installing plugins without criteria
- Using bloated themes
- Not understanding the hosting environment
- Updating without checking compatibility
- Not auditing before intervening

None of these problems are the fault of the CMS.



---

## Well-Handled WordPress is a Solid Platform

A well-configured WordPress can be:

- Fast
- Secure
- Scalable
- Optimized for SEO and GEO
- Easy to maintain

Large corporations, governments, and major media outlets use it correctly every day.

---

## What You Should Do Before Rebuilding Your Web

Before switching technologies, **audit your current site**.

An audit reveals:
- What is actually wrong
- What can be saved
- What is worth improving
- What is NOT worth touching
