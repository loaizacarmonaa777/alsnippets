## Update Best Practices.

Updating is necessary.

But updating without control can break your site.

Many owners live between two extremes:  
they never update at all, or they update everything at once.

Neither is ideal.

---

### Why it happens

It happens because:

- There is no staging environment
- There are no reliable backups
- Compatibilities are not checked
- Updating is done without planning

WordPress recommends keeping the system updated to avoid vulnerabilities.

The key is in how it's done.

---

### What it is

Best practices mean:

- Backup before any change
- Updates in stages
- Compatibility testing
- Post-update monitoring

It's not just pressing a button.

It's about managing risk.

---

### What it affects

Updating poorly can cause:

- Critical errors
- Plugin conflicts
- Performance drops
- Loss of features

Not updating at all can open vulnerabilities.

Balance is found in a controlled process.

---

### Possible solutions

When I manage updates at Alsnippets, I do:

- Prior full backup
- Testing in a controlled environment
- Progressive updating
- Subsequent functional validation

Then I organize a maintenance calendar and solve potential conflicts.

Updating shouldn't be scary.  
It should be done with a method.
