## Visual Stability and Interactive Experience.

Nothing generates more frustration than trying to click...

And having the button move.

Or having the content jump.

Or having something appear late and mess everything up.

That's not just a visual detail.

It's a loss of trust.

A stable site transmits control.

An unstable site transmits improvisation.

---

### What it's about

Visual stability refers to avoiding unexpected changes in the layout while the page loads.

This is what Google measures as CLS (Cumulative Layout Shift).

But beyond the metric, it's about experience.

The user needs predictability.

If the interface changes without warning, the interaction breaks.

---

### Why it happens

It happens because:

- Image dimensions are not defined
- Late ads are loaded
- Dynamic elements are inserted without reserved space
- Web fonts generate reflow
- Scripts alter the DOM after rendering

Many developments do not reserve space.

And that produces jumps.



---

### What it is

Working on visual stability involves:

- Defining explicit sizes for images and containers
- Avoiding late insertions without space reservation
- Optimizing font loading
- Controlling invasive animations
- Minimizing DOM reflow

At Alsnippets, I organize the layout to be predictable.

I don't let the design depend on chance.

Google documents CLS at  
https://web.dev/cls/

---

### What it affects

When there is visual instability:

- Frustration increases
- Accidental clicks are generated
- Bounce rate goes up
- Conversion goes down

And it also affects experience metrics.

But most seriously, it erodes trust.

A site that moves on its own does not convey professionalism.

---

### Possible solutions

When I solve stability problems at Alsnippets, I do:

- Real CLS audit
- Image loading review
- Strategic space reservation
- Web font optimization
- Elimination of intrusive scripts

Then I produce a fluid experience.

No jumps.

No surprises.

You can complement this with  
[Technical dependency audit](/en/auditoria-tecnica-de-dependencias)

---

### Tips

- Always define width and height in images
- Be careful with heavy sliders
- Don't insert banners without reserved space
- Measure behavior on mobile, not just desktop

Stability is not seen.

But it is felt.

And the user appreciates it.
