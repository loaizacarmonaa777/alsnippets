## Safe Restoration if Necessary.

Sometimes the best solution is not cleaning.

It is restoring.

But restoring incorrectly can bring the problem back to the exact same point.

I have seen sites get re-infected because the backup was already compromised.

---

### Why it happens

It happens because:

- The exact start of the infection is unknown
- Automatic copies already include malware
- The backup state is not verified

Restoring without validation is like copying a virus onto another drive.

WordPress recommends verifying integrity before recovering.



---

### What it is

Restoring safely involves:

- Identifying the last clean point
- Analyzing files before uploading them
- Verifying the database
- Confirming the absence of backdoors

It's not simply "clicking restore."

It's validating first.

---

### What it affects

An incorrect restoration can:

- Reinstall malware
- Reopen vulnerabilities
- Damage site integrity
- Lead to the loss of recent data

And the cycle starts all over again.



---

### Possible solutions

When I handle restorations at Alsnippets, I do:

- Identification of the infection date
- Scanning the backup before uploading it
- Restoration in an isolated environment
- Post-restoration manual validation
- Immediate security reinforcement

Then I organize access, solve vulnerabilities, and produce a stable version.

Restoring is not going backward.  
It is rebuilding correctly.

I also recommend reviewing [Access and Permissions Reinforcement](/en/refuerzo-accesos-permisos).
