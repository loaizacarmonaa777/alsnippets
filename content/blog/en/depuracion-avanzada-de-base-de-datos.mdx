## Advanced Database Debugging.

WordPress runs thanks to its database.

But almost nobody checks it.

Over time, it fills up with:

- Unnecessary revisions
- Automatically loaded options (autoload)
- Orphaned tables
- Poorly optimized queries

And that ends up affecting everything.

---

### Why it happens

It happens because every plugin leaves a trace.

Even if you delete them, they often leave tables or records behind.

If the site has been running for years, the database can become saturated.

This directly impacts the response time.



---

### What it is

Debugging the database involves:

- Optimizing tables
- Reviewing heavy queries
- Cleaning up obsolete records
- Adjusting indexes
- Verifying unnecessary autoload options

It’s not about deleting everything.

It’s about intervening with professional criteria.

---

### What it affects

A cluttered database can generate:

- General slowness
- Resource consumption spikes
- Intermittent errors
- Crashes under high traffic

Even if you optimize images or scripts, the problem will remain on the inside.

---

### Possible solutions

When I tackle debugging at Alsnippets, I do:

- A complete structural analysis
- Cleanup of unnecessary tables
- Query optimization
- Autoload adjustment
